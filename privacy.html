Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026
This is the privacy policy for the personal portfolio website of Zainab Owoilu, located at olazee04.github.io/portfolio. This page explains what information this site collects, how it is used, and what rights you have. This is a personal portfolio site and is not operated for commercial purposes.

What information is collected

This portfolio website does not use cookies, tracking scripts or analytics tools. It does not collect any personal data automatically when you visit.

The only personal information collected is what you voluntarily provide when you use the contact form. This includes:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The subject and content of your message

How your information is used

Any information you submit through the contact form is used solely to respond to your message. Your contact details will not be shared with third parties, sold, or used for marketing purposes of any kind.

Messages submitted through the form are sent directly to the site owner's email address and are not stored in any database.

Third-party services

This website uses the following third-party services that may have their own privacy practices:

  • Google Fonts : used to load the typefaces on this site. Google may collect data when fonts are loaded. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages : this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect basic access logs. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

No advertising networks, tracking pixels or social media embeds are used on this site.

External links

This portfolio contains links to external websites including GitHub and live project deployments. Once you leave this site, this privacy policy no longer applies. Please review the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

Your rights

Since this site does not store personal data beyond contact form messages, there is very little data held about you. However, if you have submitted a message and would like it deleted, or if you have any questions about how your information has been handled, you can contact me directly.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the site after any changes means you accept the updated policy.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, feel free to get in touch directly.
